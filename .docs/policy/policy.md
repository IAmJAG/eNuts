## Overview & Design Context

This directory documents the policy (model) component of eNuts.

The policy accepts a current intent, a stack of sequential streaming images, and a stack of previously taken actions with their arguments, then produces the next discrete action together with the arguments that action should use.

* **Design Decision / Deviation Notes:** This is a redesign. The earlier architecture sketch lives at `.docs/hybrid_discrete_continuous_policy.md` and is retained only for historical reference. All current requirements are defined in `requirement.md` within this directory.
